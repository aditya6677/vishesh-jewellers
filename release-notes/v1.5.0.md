# KaratDesk 1.5.0

## Fine Gold
- Track Fine Gold stock independently of jewellery Gold and Silver.
- Sell Fine Gold through invoices, with stock weight and quantity deductions and edit/void/delete reversals.
- Show separate Fine Gold inventory and sales totals on the dashboard and in the full Excel export.
- Existing 24K and other Gold records remain unchanged unless explicitly edited. Refining/conversion is not included.

## Improvements
- Use KaratDesk as the default logo; explicitly uploaded store logos remain unchanged.
- Keep onboarding visible until the main workspace loads, avoiding the empty setup-to-app gap.
- Fix the hidden third stock card and tolerate older backend responses without Fine Gold totals.
- Match Legal & Support spacing to other Settings sections.
- Add an optional database development watcher with automatic frontend builds and backend restarts.

Back up your data before upgrading. Installers are unsigned; Windows or macOS may display a publisher warning. Existing encrypted data locations and installation identity are retained. Legacy JSON import is not included.
