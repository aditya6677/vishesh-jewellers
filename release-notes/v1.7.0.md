# KaratDesk 1.7.0

## Inventory and invoicing
- Track Fine Silver separately from jewellery Silver, including stock cards, invoice sales, dashboard summaries and Excel exports. Existing Silver records are not automatically reclassified.
- Show read-only Less Weight as gross minus net weight in invoice entry and printed/PDF invoices.
- Add optional Other Charges per invoice item, defaulting to zero. Charges are added once per line after making charges and before GST; pieces still affect inventory quantity only.
- Add B2B tax treatment with required customer GSTIN, separate from the store GSTIN, printed on invoices and retained during editing and regeneration.
- Label non-GST bills as Estimate Invoice without changing their calculation.
- Create stock from an unsuccessful invoice stock search using the shared stock form, with permission checks, validation and automatic selection of the newly created stock ID.
- Capitalize the first letter of stock-name words on creation and editing. Existing names remain unchanged unless edited; abbreviations retain their remaining letter case.

## Interface improvements
- Refine invoice typography, spacing, numeric columns, print styling and the totals panel.
- Place customer GSTIN beside Tax Treatment on larger screens, with responsive stacking.
- Use a centered, compact two-column Superadmin setup page on desktop.
- Add page-level progress overlays for store setup, account creation, sign-in and workspace opening, with error recovery and reduced-motion support.

## Upgrade and recovery
Back up existing data and keep the recovery passphrase before installing.
Accounts, licensing and the internal encrypted-data identity remain compatible.
Legacy encrypted JSON import still requires access to its original matching key and OS secure-storage context. For cross-computer transfer, export a passphrase-protected portable legacy file on the original computer; copying an OS-protected key file alone is not a portable backup.

Installers are unsigned. Verify the download source and SHA256 checksums; operating systems may display publisher warnings. Never disable antivirus or delete original data to bypass a recovery error.
