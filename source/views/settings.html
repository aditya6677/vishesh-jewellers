Encrypted Backups

A backup is created automatically once per week when the app starts. You can also create one whenever you want or restore a previous backup. Weekly backups are saved outside the app installation folder, in Documents → Vishesh Jewellers Backups.

The business data in the backup is encrypted. Restore requires the matching app encryption key.

Export All Data

Download a readable Excel workbook with a Business Snapshot dashboard first, followed by separate sheets for invoices, invoice items, exchanges, settlements, returns, stock, item catalog, purchases, payments, and the old-metal ledger. A complete-data sheet is included as a portable reference.

The Excel file is not encrypted and contains customer details. Store it securely.
